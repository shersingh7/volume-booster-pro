# Signal Chassis — Design Philosophy

**Signal Chassis** is the visual movement for Volume Booster Pro: warm industrial material language meeting analog measurement precision. Form follows the logic of a studio rack module, not a software dashboard. Every surface reads as machined plate, bezel, or indicator—never as a floating card or glass panel.

**Space** is deliberate and quiet. Generous optical margins and sparse composition let the control surface breathe. Marketing canvases treat the popup as a photographed instrument: large enough to inspect, framed by abstract browser geometry rather than cluttered mockups. Empty field is a design material, not leftover canvas.

**Color** is a three-note chord. Warm bone chassis carries near-black LCD and hardware mass; fluorescent signal orange marks active gain and attention only. Secondary LEDs (green, amber, red) stay tiny and state-bound. No cool SaaS blues, no purple gradients, no glow soup.

**Rhythm** is metrical and hardware-like. Alignments, notches, and scales imply calibration marks. Type is condensed, uppercase where labels read as silkscreen, tabular where numbers measure. Headlines stay minimal and truthful—captions for an instrument, not marketing copy walls.

**Craftsmanship** favors hard edges, tiny radii, and tactile affordances over soft ornament. Logos and icons keep bold silhouettes with centered safe geometry so Chrome crops still leave a legible mark at 16px. Screenshots are composed like editorial product photography: one campaign, three distinct gain states, zero remote assets or false claims.
